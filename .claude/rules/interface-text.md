# Interface text

- Plain words a newcomer understands. Explain what a thing is before giving its numbers.
- Sentence case. British spelling: colour, licence (noun), kilometre.
- No jargon or bare abbreviations: write the long form first, then the short one in brackets.
- Units in words people use: km, km/h, °C, MW. Numbers through `num()`.
- State where the data comes from and how old it is when that matters.
- Errors say what happened and what still works. They do not apologise.
